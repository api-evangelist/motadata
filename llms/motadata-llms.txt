# Motadata: AI-Powered Observability and IT Service Management

> Motadata (Mindarray Systems) is a global IT software company that helps organizations run, secure, and automate their IT operations through two AI-native platforms built on a shared DFIT deep-learning foundation. ObserveOps (formerly known as AIOps) is a unified observability platform that brings metrics, logs, traces, flows, and topology into one backend with adaptive AI that needs no training period. ServiceOps is an ITIL-aligned IT and enterprise service management suite covering service desk, asset management, patch management, and workflow automation. Because both products share the same core, an observability alert in ObserveOps can automatically open, route, and close a ticket in ServiceOps.

This file is a curated, human-organised map of Motadata's product, capability, documentation, and reference pages, intended for consumption by LLMs and AI agents. Every link is a current, on-topic page.

## Products

- [ObserveOps](https://www.motadata.com/products/observeops): Unified observability platform (formerly known as AIOps) correlating metrics, logs, traces, flows, and topology, with anomaly detection and multiple on-prem and cloud deployment modes.
- [ServiceOps](https://www.motadata.com/products/serviceops): AI-powered, ITIL-aligned ITSM suite with service desk, CMDB, asset management, patch management, and automation.

## ObserveOps Modules

- [Network Observability](https://www.motadata.com/products/network-monitoring-tool): Availability and performance monitoring for network devices.
- [Network Configuration & Compliance Management (NCCM)](https://www.motadata.com/products/network-automation-tool): Configuration and change automation for network infrastructure.
- [Hybrid Infrastructure Monitoring](https://www.motadata.com/products/it-infrastructure-monitoring-tool): Unified visibility across servers, networks, and applications.
- [Log Monitoring](https://www.motadata.com/products/log-analyzer): Centralised log collection, search, and analysis.
- [Application Performance Monitoring](https://www.motadata.com/products/application-performance-monitoring): Tracing and performance monitoring for applications and services.

- [Service Level Objectives](https://www.motadata.com/products/service-level-objectives): Defining and tracking SLOs for service delivery.

## ServiceOps Modules

- [IT Asset & Configuration Management](https://www.motadata.com/products/it-asset-management): Discovery and lifecycle management of IT assets.
